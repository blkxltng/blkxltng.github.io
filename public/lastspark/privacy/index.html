Last Spark Privacy Policy

Effective October 20, 2026

This policy explains how the Last Spark game ("the Game") for Android and iOS handles information. The Game is made by Maurice Gaynor ("I", "me"), an independent developer.

In short: I don't collect, store or sell any personal information about you. The Game shows ads through Google AdMob, and Google may collect some device information to show and measure those ads, as described below.

Information I collect

None. The Game has no accounts or sign-in, and it doesn't send me your name, email address, contacts, precise location or any other personal information. I don't run my own analytics or tracking.

Information stored on your device

Your best scores, daily streaks, unlocked themes, settings and similar game progress are saved only on your device. This data never leaves your device, and I can't see it. Uninstalling the Game deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game is free and supported by ads from Google AdMob. Ads appear on the title screen and occasionally between runs, never during play. To serve, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may automatically collect information such as:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID or Apple's IDFA, where permitted);
  • your IP address and general location derived from it;
  • device and app information, such as model, operating system, language and app version;
  • how you interact with ads, such as views and taps;
  • diagnostic information about ad performance.

Google handles this information under its own policies. You can learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your ad choices

  • Consent: where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and some US states), the Game shows Google's consent message before any ad loads. You can choose whether ads may be personalised. You can change your choice at any time from Options → Privacy Choices in the Game.
  • iOS: your advertising identifier is used only if you allow it through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can change this in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact menu varies by device).
  • Google ad settings: you can manage ad personalisation at My Ad Center.

If you choose non-personalised ads, you will still see ads, but they won't be based on your past activity.

In-app purchases

If the Game offers purchases (such as removing ads), they are processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. I don't receive your payment details. Those stores' own privacy policies apply.

Sharing your runs

When you tap SHARE, the Game creates an image of your run and hands it to your device's share menu. Where it goes is up to you, and I don't receive a copy.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, please contact me and I will help address it.

Your rights

Because I don't hold personal information about you, there is nothing for me to access, correct or delete. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or state privacy laws in the US), you may have rights over data that Google collects for advertising. You can exercise them through the Privacy Choices option in the Game, your device settings or Google's tools linked above. I don't sell or share your personal information.

Changes to this policy

If the Game adds new features that handle information differently, such as online leaderboards, I will update this page and change the effective date above.
