Last Spark Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026

This policy explains how the Last Spark game ("the Game") for Android and iOS handles information. The Game is made by blkxltng ("I", "me"), an independent developer.

In short: I don't collect, store or sell any personal information about you. The Game shows ads through Google AdMob, and Google may collect some device information to show and measure those ads. If you choose to sign in to Google Play Games or Game Center, your scores and achievements go to Google or Apple for the leaderboards. Details are below.

Information I collect

None. I don't run my own servers, analytics or tracking, and the Game doesn't send me your name, email address, contacts, precise location or any other personal information. The Game has no accounts of its own.

Information stored on your device

Your best scores, daily streaks, unlocked themes, achievements, settings and similar game progress are saved on your device. Uninstalling the Game deletes them. If you sign in to Google Play Games or Game Center, some of this is also sent to Google or Apple, as described below. I can't see it either way.

Leaderboards and achievements (Google Play Games and Game Center)

The Game offers leaderboards and achievements through Google Play Games on Android and Game Center on iOS. They're optional: the Game works fully without signing in. When you're signed in, the Game sends your scores, Daily Galaxy and Rekindle results, and unlocked achievements to Google or Apple, which show them on leaderboards along with your gamer name and avatar. Google and Apple handle this information under their own privacy policies (the Google Privacy Policy and Apple's Privacy Policy), and you can manage or delete it in your Play Games profile or your Game Center settings. I don't receive your account details.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game is free and supported by ads from Google AdMob. Ads appear on the title screen and occasionally between runs, never during play. To serve, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may automatically collect information such as:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID or Apple's IDFA, where permitted);
  • your IP address and general location derived from it;
  • device and app information, such as model, operating system, language and app version;
  • how you interact with ads, such as views and taps;
  • diagnostic information about ad performance.

Google handles this information under its own policies. You can learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your ad choices

  • Consent: where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and some US states), the Game shows Google's consent message before any ad loads. You can choose whether ads may be personalized. You can change your choice at any time from Options → Privacy Choices in the Game.
  • iOS: your advertising identifier is used only if you allow it through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can change this in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact menu varies by device).
  • Google ad settings: you can manage ad personalization at My Ad Center.

If you choose non-personalized ads, you will still see ads, but they won't be based on your past activity.

In-app purchases

The Game offers one optional purchase that removes the ads. It's processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store, and I don't receive your payment details. The Game only checks with the store whether you own it, and remembers the answer on your device. Those stores' own privacy policies apply.

Sharing your runs

When you tap SHARE, the Game creates an image of your run and hands it to your device's share menu. Where it goes is up to you, and I don't receive a copy.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, please contact me and I will help address it.

Your rights

Because I don't hold personal information about you, there is nothing for me to access, correct or delete. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or state privacy laws in the US), you may have rights over data that Google collects for advertising, or that Google and Apple hold for leaderboards and achievements. You can exercise them through the Privacy Choices option in the Game, your device settings, your Play Games profile or Game Center settings, or Google's and Apple's tools linked above. I don't sell or share your personal information.

Changes to this policy

If the Game adds new features that handle information differently, I will update this page and change the effective date above.
